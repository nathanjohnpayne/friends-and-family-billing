# Year-End Review

Covers the guided page through which a new billing year is created: the closing year's bill changes (True-ups and Service Credits), subscriptions that started mid-year, member charges, the per-household preview, the atomic rollover that appends the review's records to the closing year and seeds the new year with an itemized carry, the local draft, the next-year guard, and the member-facing "Carried from &lt;year&gt;" section. Decisions: [ADR 0009](../docs/adr/0009-year-end-review-true-ups-and-itemized-carry.md). Wireframes: `plans/ffb-wireframes.html` (`#fx-year-end-…`, `#fx-settings-populated-…`, `#fx-dashboard-yearend-…`, `#fx-share-carried-…`, `#fx-invoicing-preview-carried-…`, `#fx-dlg-email-invoice-full-carried-…`, `#fx-dlg-bill-history-rollover-…`).

Out of scope for v1: standalone mid-year price changes or late starts (the pure builders stay callable), mid-year membership changes on a bill, frequency changes as part of a price change, day-level proration or non-calendar years, new UI for off-cycle billing True-ups, effective-dated pricing or price history on the bill model.

## Acceptance Criteria

### Vocabulary and units

- Administrator-facing copy (the review page, billing events, the bill history dialog) may say True-up. Member-facing copy (share page, invoice preview, invoice email, text invoice) never says True-up, carry_opening, reconciliation, or adjustment; the approved section heading is **Carried from &lt;year&gt;** and every line says what happened and when (see [Member-facing copy](#member-facing-copy)).
- **Money is stored in dollars as JavaScript numbers rounded to cents**, the existing persisted convention (`amount: shareCents / 100`). Arithmetic is done in integer cents and converted once: `toCents(x) = Math.round((x + Number.EPSILON) * 100)`, the convention `recordUsageCharge` and `recordServiceCredit` already use.
- **Signs.** An `owedAdjustments[]` record's `amount` is a positive magnitude; `kind` gives the direction (`usage_charge` raises owed, `service_credit` lowers it). A `carry_opening` seed's `amount` is signed (a carried charge positive, a carried credit negative), as today. `items[].amount` and `detail[].amount` are signed the same way (charge `+`, credit `−`). Preview and display totals are signed; the UI renders `+$12.00` and `−$26.00`.
- `calc` stores the amounts the administrator entered in the bill's own frequency plus the derived monthly figures: `{ frequency: 'monthly' | 'annual', fromAmount, toAmount, fromMonthly, toMonthly, firstMonth, lastMonth, months, computed, gross, overridden }`. `fromMonthly`/`toMonthly` are `amount` for a monthly bill and `amount / 12` **unrounded** for an annual bill; `firstMonth` / `lastMonth` are `YYYY-MM` strings inside the source year (`lastMonth` only for `basis: 'ended'`, `fromAmount`/`fromMonthly` null for `basis: 'late_start'`); `months` is the count the formula used; `computed` is the formula's result in dollars; `gross` is the amount actually recorded (equal to `computed` unless `overridden: true`); the per-member records sum exactly to `gross`.

### True-up and Service Credit math (`src/lib/year-end-review.js`, pure)

- `monthsFromFirst(firstMonth)` returns `13 − month(firstMonth)` (July → 6, January → 12, December → 1). `monthsAfterLast(lastMonth)` returns `12 − month(lastMonth)` (August → 4, December → 0). Both reject a month outside the source year.
- **Price change.** `grossCents = toCents((toMonthly − fromMonthly) × months)` with `months = monthsFromFirst(firstMonth)`. A positive result is a True-up (`kind: 'usage_charge'`, `basis: 'price_change'`); a negative result is a Service Credit (`kind: 'service_credit'`, `basis: 'price_change'`) of the absolute value. For an annual bill `toMonthly − fromMonthly = (toAmount − fromAmount) / 12`, multiplied by `months` **before** rounding: the annual-increase fixture is $16.67, not $16.70.
- **Late start.** `grossCents = toCents(toMonthly × months)` with `months = monthsFromFirst(firstMonth)`; a True-up with `basis: 'late_start'`.
- **Ended.** `grossCents = toCents(fromMonthly × monthsAfterLast(lastMonth))`; a Service Credit with `basis: 'ended'`. The last month of service is included in what was used; the credit covers the following month through December.
- **Already credited.** A bill marked Ended or Price changed (downward) may already carry active Service Credits recorded during the year from the Bills tab or a Review Request resolution (`recordServiceCredit`, `kind: 'service_credit'`, `status: 'active'`, same `billId`). The row shows their sum ("Already credited: $48.00, recorded Sep 2") and the credit to record defaults to `max(0, computed − alreadyCredited)`, with the formula showing the subtraction; the administrator may override it (including to 0). A zero result records nothing and the bill is still left out of the new year. Per-member existing credits are summed at the bill grain; the row never voids or edits an existing credit.
- **Split.** `splitCents(totalCents, memberIds)` is one shared helper, extracted from `recordServiceCredit` with its exact behavior: `base = Math.floor(totalCents / n)`, every member but the last gets `base`, the last gets `totalCents − base × (n − 1)`, so the shares sum exactly to `totalCents`. `recordServiceCredit` calls it and its existing tests pass unchanged.
- **Override.** The administrator may replace the gross amount; the record keeps `calc.computed`, sets `calc.gross` to the override and `calc.overridden: true`, and the split is of the override. An override must be a positive amount in the same direction as the formula (a True-up cannot be overridden into a credit; use a Service Credit row instead).
- **Zero cases are explicit.** `months = 0` (ended in December) records **no** Service Credit and still leaves the bill out of the new year. A price change whose new price equals the current price is a validation error ("No change—leave the bill Unchanged or enter a different price"), never a zero-amount record. An override of 0 or less is a validation error. A late-start bill with no members is a validation error. A Service Credit on a bill whose members are empty is impossible by construction (the bill's members are the split).
- **Formula strings** shown beside every computed amount: price change `($198.00 − $180.00) × 6 months = $108.00 ÷ 9 members = $12.00 each`; annual `($159.00 − $139.00) ÷ 12 × 10 months = $16.67 ÷ 3 members = $5.55 · $5.55 · $5.57`; late start `$9.99 × 4 months = $39.96 ÷ 3 members = $13.32 each`; ended `$12.00 × 4 months (Sep–Dec) = $48.00 ÷ 3 members = $16.00 each`. An overridden row shows `computed $108.00 → entered $100.00`.

### Record shapes

- **True-up records** (one per member of the bill): `{ id: 'oadj_tu_<sourceYearId>_<trueUpId>_<memberId>', trueUpId, memberId, billId, billName, kind: 'usage_charge', basis: 'price_change' | 'late_start', amount, description, incurredDate, calc, status, createdAt, reconciliationId }`. `trueUpId` is `tu_<sourceYearId>_<billId>` for a price change and `tu_<sourceYearId>_new_<newBillId>` for a late start. `description` is the member-facing sentence (below). `incurredDate` is the first day of `firstMonth`. `status` is `'deferred'` in the in-memory reconciliation and `'carried_forward'` in the first persisted state (ADR 0009 § 3). A Usage Charge record with no `basis` is treated as `basis: 'member'` everywhere.
- **Review Service Credit records**: the existing `recordServiceCredit` shape plus `basis: 'price_change' | 'ended'`, `calc`, `reconciliationId`, `id: 'oadj_sc_<sourceYearId>_<basis>_<billId>_<memberId>'`, `reason` set to the member-facing sentence, `status: 'active'`.
- **Member charges** added in Step 3: the existing `recordUsageCharge` shape (`basis` absent) with `id: 'oadj_uc_<sourceYearId>_<rowId>'` and `reconciliationId`, where `rowId` is allocated once when the row is created in the draft and stored on it. Existing deferred charges shown in Step 3 are reused by id and never re-recorded.
- **New bills** in Step 2 allocate `billId` once with `generateUniqueBillId(existingIds)` when the row is created and store it on the draft; the True-up's `billId` and the new year's bill use that id. On draft restoration a `billId` that collides with a current source-year bill is reallocated and the row is flagged for re-check.
- **The carried credit** record on the source year uses the deterministic id `cadj_carry_<nextYearLabel>_<primaryMemberId>` (the `applyCarryForwardToPriorYear` default factory), so the review path never passes a random `idFactory`.
- **New persisted fields** on `owedAdjustments[]` records (`basis`, `calc`, `trueUpId`, `billName`, `reconciliationId`) and on `carry_opening` seeds (`items[]`) ride the verbatim array round-trip of `buildSavePayload`; no allowlist change (ADR 0008). Completion evidence lives in `billingEvents[]`, also already in the allowlist.
- No change to `firestore.rules` or `storage.rules`.

### Itemized carry (`src/lib/billing-year.js`)

- `buildCarryOpeningItems(household, sourceYear)` builds, from the ids `buildCarryForward` already returns for a household (`deferredChargeIds`, `credit`, `creditAdjustmentIds`), the seed's `items[]`: one item per carried deferred Usage Charge `{ sourceId: <charge id>, kind: 'usage_charge', basis, memberId, billName, description, amount: +charge.amount, incurredDate, calc }` (True-ups included, `basis: 'price_change' | 'late_start'`; member charges `basis: 'member'`), and, when the household carries a Credit, exactly one `{ sourceId: 'cadj_carry_<nextYearLabel>_<primaryMemberId>', kind: 'credit', basis: 'credit', memberId: <primary>, billName: null, description: 'Your household's <year> credit', amount: −credit, detail: [...] }`. `src/lib/calculations.js` is unchanged.
- `detail[]` provenance: `buildCarryForward` returns no Service Credit ids, so the review path passes its own Service Credit records to `buildCarryOpeningItems` through `ctx.reviewServiceCredits` (the records built for this `reconciliationId`, before they are appended), and the helper filters them by the household's member ids. Service Credits recorded earlier in the year (from the Bills tab or a Review Request) are never listed in `detail[]`; they are part of the remainder line. The legacy path passes none and produces no detail.
- `detail[]` on the credit item lists the review's Service Credits for the household's members as non-additive explanation: `{ sourceId, basis, memberId, billName, description, amount: −share }`, plus one closing explanatory line when their sum differs from the credit: `{ kind: 'remainder', description: 'Other <year> overpayment', amount: −(credit − sum) }` when the credit exceeds them, or `{ kind: 'applied', description: 'Part applied to your <year> balance', amount: +(sum − credit) }` when they exceed the credit. Detail never contributes to the seed's amount or to any total.
- **Invariant:** for every seed, `Σ items[].amount` equals the seed's signed `amount` within `CREDIT_EPSILON`. A property test over generated fixtures (random households, charges, credits, True-ups, Service Credits) asserts it, and asserts that `detail[]` is excluded from the sum.
- Each item snapshots `billName` and `description` at rollover; renderers never look up the new year's bill names or prices for carried items.
- `buildNewYearData(..., carry, fromYearLabel)` writes `items` on each seed when the carry supplies them; a carry without items (the legacy call) seeds exactly as today. Seeds written before this change have no `items`.
- Attribution: a True-up or member charge item is attributed to its `memberId` (a linked member's item shows under that member within the household); the credit item is attributed to the household, never to one member as though it were that member's charge.
- **A carried credit larger than the year's bills is preserved.** `getHouseholdFinancials` floors owed at 0, so a household whose carried credit exceeds its bill-derived owed (an ended bill, or a year with no bills) reads owed 0 and credit 0, and `buildCarryForward` would carry nothing at the next rollover: the excess would vanish. `buildCarryForwardSummary` in `billing-year.js` therefore computes each household's floor residual from the values `getHouseholdFinancials` already returns, `floorResidual = max(0, −(grossOwed − serviceCreditTotal + priorOpeningBalance))`, adds it to the household's carryable `credit` and `openingBalance`, and `buildCarryOpeningItems` records it on the credit item's `detail[]` as `{ kind: 'unused', description: 'Unused <year> credit', amount: −floorResidual }`. `src/lib/calculations.js` is unchanged. The preservation is applied on the review path only (`buildCarryForwardSummary(..., { preserveFloorResidual: true })`), so the legacy `createYear` call stays byte-for-byte as today (ADR 0009 § 3); because the review is the only UI path that creates a year, the #322 edge case no longer reaches users. Member-facing surfaces show a $0.00 total for such a household with the line "Your <year> credit of $X covers this year; $Y remains."
- Fixture 9 (excess credit): a fully paid household whose only $12/mo bill ended in January carries −$132.00 into a year with no bills; that year's first invoice is $0.00 with $132.00 remaining; at that year's rollover the household carries −$132.00 again (one credit item, `detail[]` with the `unused` line). A variant with one $5/mo bill ($60/yr) in the new year carries −$72.00.

### Next-year bills

- `deriveNextYearBills(sourceBills, reviewBills, newBills)` returns the new year's `bills[]`: every source bill not marked Ended, with `amount` set to its next-year price (defaulting to the new price when the price changed, otherwise the current price; always editable), the same `billingFrequency`, members, logo, and website; plus each new subscription as a bill with the draft's `billId`, name, amount, frequency, website, and members. Ended bills are absent.
- The source year's `bills[]` is never changed: no amount edits, no late-start bills added.
- A next-year price must be a positive number in the bill's frequency; an increase announced for January is entered here without a True-up.

### Household preview (Step 4)

- `buildHouseholdPreview(...)` returns, per household (primary plus linked members, ADR 0001): `{ primaryMemberId, members: [{ memberId, sourceBillTotal, nextBillTotal }], sourceBillTotal, nextBillTotal, subscriptionDelta, items: [...], carriedTotal, firstInvoiceTotal }` where `sourceBillTotal` is the household's **bill-derived annual total in the closing year** (`calculateAnnualSummary` over the source bills, before any adjustment), `nextBillTotal` is the same over the next-year bills, `subscriptionDelta = nextBillTotal − sourceBillTotal`, `carriedTotal = Σ items[].amount`, and `firstInvoiceTotal = max(0, nextBillTotal + carriedTotal)` (the floor mirrors `getHouseholdFinancials`; a residual credit beyond the first invoice rides the normal credit path).
- The preview labels its baseline explicitly: "2026 bills" (what the household was billed for 2026 subscriptions) versus "2027 bills" (next year's subscriptions at their new prices). It never compares an unpaid balance with an annual billed cost, and it shows **Subscriptions vs 2026** separately from **Carried from 2026** so a catch-up charge is never hidden inside a price change.
- Every computed number shows its formula on demand; nothing is written until Start.

### The page (`/year-end-review`, `src/app/views/YearEndReview/YearEndReviewView.jsx`)

- A page under the app shell, not a dialog. Four steps with a step indicator; each step validates on Next and shows errors inline; Back never loses input. Steps 1–3 can be skipped only through the explicit "Nothing changed" confirmations (below).
- **Step 1 — Bills.** Every source bill, defaulting to **Unchanged**, with a one-click **No price changes this year**. Per row: Price changed (new price in the bill's frequency, first month billed at it, the computed True-up or Service Credit with formula and per-member split, amount overridable), Ended (last month of service, the computed Service Credit, "left out of 2027"), and **Next year's price** for every bill (default current or new price). A price change from January shows the hint "This bills all twelve months at the new price."
- **Step 2 — New subscriptions.** Empty by default and fast to skip. A row: name, price, frequency, website (optional, for the logo), members, first month of service; shows the late-start True-up and the next-year bill it creates.
- **Step 3 — Member charges.** Per household, the existing deferred Usage Charges prefilled (read-only, "already recorded") and **Add charge** with the existing Usage Charge validation (positive amount, description required, member of the household). Charges added here are recorded in the batch, not immediately.
- **Step 4 — Review & start &lt;year&gt;.** Per household: 2026 bills, 2027 bills at their new prices with the subscription delta, carried items grouped by member with the household credit (and its detail) at the household level, the carried total, the first-invoice total; each household expandable; Edit links back to Steps 1–3. Then the year label (from `suggestNextYearLabel`, editable, duplicate-checked with `isYearLabelDuplicate`), a plain-language statement of exactly what Start will do (counts of True-ups, Service Credits, member charges, new bills, price changes, ended bills, households carrying, and the two documents written), and **Start &lt;year&gt;**.
- **Nothing changed** is a fast path that requires three explicit confirmations: no closing-year bill changes, no new subscriptions, no additional member charges. "No price changes" alone does not skip Steps 2 and 3. It skips the editing of Steps 1–3; Step 4 still shows every existing deferred charge, Credit, next-year price (defaulting to the current price), and resulting total, with links back to edit. It persists no new financial records merely because a row was prefilled; prefilled deferred Usage Charges are carried once, never re-recorded.
- Entry points: Settings → Billing year (**Review & start &lt;next year&gt;**, replacing Start New Year and its label dialog), and a Dashboard prompt card when no next year exists and either the year is Closed or today's date is on or after November 1 of the year's numeric label (both triggers; a non-numeric label uses the Closed trigger only).
- Guard: `findSuccessorYear(billingYears, sourceYear, sourceEvents)` returns a successor when (1) the source year's `billingEvents` contain a `YEAR_ROLLED_OVER` or `YEAR_CARRIED_FORWARD` event (its `toYear`), or (2) a billing year exists whose numeric label is greater than the source's numeric label; otherwise null. The review is offered only when there is no successor and the source status is `open`, `settling`, or `closed` (never `archived`). The guard runs at entry and again at Start after `_loadYearsList()`, and the commit precondition is the final check. When a next year exists the page says so and points to recording new charges in the open year (ADR 0007).

### Draft (local only)

- Draft state is component state mirrored to `localStorage` under `ffb.yearEndReview.v1.<uid>.<sourceYearId>`; every read and write is wrapped in `try/catch`; the billing-year document is never written before Start (ADR 0008 untouched).
- Shape: `{ draftVersion: 1, reconciliationId, userId, sourceYearId, sourceLabel, nextYearLabel, sourceFingerprint, savedAt, bills: { [billId]: { change: 'unchanged' | 'price_change' | 'ended', newAmount, firstMonth, lastMonth, override, nextYearAmount } }, newBills: [{ rowId, billId, name, amount, frequency, website, members, firstMonth, override }], charges: [{ rowId, memberId, amount, description, incurredDate }], confirmations: { noBillChanges, noNewSubscriptions, noExtraCharges } }`. `reconciliationId` is allocated once when the draft is created and never changes through restoration or retries. Derived amounts (True-ups, Service Credits, items, totals) are never stored.
- `validateDraft(raw, { userId, sourceYear, bills, familyMembers })` treats storage as untrusted input: the shape and every field type are checked; a draft for another user, another source year, or another `draftVersion` is incompatible and discarded with a message; referenced bills and members are resolved against current data (a bill that no longer exists drops its row with a notice; a member no longer on a bill or in the household drops that charge with a notice); amounts and months are re-validated; derived amounts are recomputed. Restoration preserves every `rowId`, `billId`, and the `reconciliationId`.
- A restored draft whose `sourceFingerprint` differs from the current fingerprint is **stale**: inputs are kept, derived amounts are recomputed, and a banner says what changed in plain terms (counts of payments, charges, credits, bills, members that differ) and that totals were recomputed. The administrator must pass through Step 4 again before Start.
- When storage is unavailable the draft degrades to in-memory state with a notice ("This draft is not saved on this device"); the page works unchanged.
- **Discard draft** is offered on every step and confirms. The draft is cleared only after confirmed success or explicit discard; a failed or uncertain commit retains it.
- When a next year was created elsewhere during the flow (detected at Start or on reload by the guard), the page says so, names the year, keeps the draft, and offers Discard draft or Open &lt;year&gt;; it never commits.

### Rollover (`BillingYearService.createYear(yearId, { reopenedAdjustmentIds, reconciliation })`)

- **Compatibility boundary.** `reconciliation` omitted (`undefined`) is the legacy call: one `writeBatch`, the same two documents, no new records, no new events, and every existing test passes unchanged. `reconciliation` present, even `{}` or a "Nothing changed" payload, is the review path. The two are distinguished by `'reconciliation' in options`, not by truthiness.
- Payload: `{ reconciliationId, sourceYearId, sourceFingerprint, trueUps: [...bill-level inputs...], serviceCredits: [...], usageCharges: [...], billOverrides: { [billId]: nextYearAmount }, endedBillIds: [...], newBills: [...], confirmations }`; the service rebuilds every record through the pure library (never trusting amounts from the page) and rejects a payload that fails validation before any write.
- Order inside the enqueued commit: (1) build the appended source-year arrays (`owedAdjustments` + True-ups as `deferred` + Service Credits + new Usage Charges); (2) `buildCarryForwardSummary` over **that** updated state with `reopenedAdjustmentIds`; (3) `applyCarryForwardToPriorYear` with the deterministic id factory, which flips every carried deferred charge (new True-ups included) to `carried_forward`; (4) `buildNewYearData` with the derived next-year bills and the itemized carry; (5) append events; (6) commit both documents atomically. True-ups are therefore never persisted as `deferred`.
- Events on the source year: `TRUE_UP_RECORDED` per True-up `{ trueUpId, billId, billName, basis, calc, amount: gross, memberCount, memberIds, reconciliationId }`; `SERVICE_CREDIT_RECORDED` (existing payload plus `basis`, `calc`, `reconciliationId`); `USAGE_CHARGE_RECORDED` (existing payload plus `reconciliationId`); `YEAR_CARRIED_FORWARD` (existing, plus `reconciliationId` and `itemCount`) when anything carries; always `YEAR_ROLLED_OVER { toYear, reconciliationId, trueUpCount, serviceCreditCount, usageChargeCount, priceChangeCount, endedCount, newBillCount }`. Events on the new year: `YEAR_CREATED_FROM_REVIEW { fromYear, reconciliationId, householdCount, carriedTotal }`; `BILL_PRICE_ROLLED_OVER { billId, billName, previousValue, newValue, billingFrequency, fromYear }` per bill whose next-year price differs from its source price; `BILL_CREATED` per new bill with `source: 'year_end_review'` and `firstMonth`. `BILLING_EVENT_LABELS` gains `TRUE_UP_RECORDED: 'True-up recorded'`, `YEAR_ROLLED_OVER: 'Year rolled over'`, `YEAR_CREATED_FROM_REVIEW: 'Year created'`, `BILL_PRICE_ROLLED_OVER: 'Price set at rollover'`.
- The review path writes the source-year document even when the source year is Closed (the existing rollover exception) and even when nothing carries (the `YEAR_ROLLED_OVER` event), so any concurrent rollover changes the source document.
- Permitted source statuses: `open`, `settling`, `closed`; `archived` is rejected. Service Credits on a Closed source year are permitted under ADR 0009's same-commit disposition rule; the service asserts, before commit, that no household's settlement status changes as a result of the review's Service Credits (a reduction can only produce a carried surplus).
- `billDeferredCharges` on a household holding a deferred True-up (the standalone-primitive case) bills it like any other deferred Usage Charge; a test confirms it.

### Commit guard and recovery (decided 2026-11-04: a Firestore transaction for the review path)

- The review path commits through `runTransaction`. Inside the callback, in this order: `tx.get` the destination (`users/<uid>/billingYears/<yearId>`) and `tx.get` the source year **before any write**; if the destination exists, throw `NEXT_YEAR_EXISTS` (never overwrite); recompute `computeSourceFingerprint(normalizeYearData(sourceSnapshot))` from the transaction's own read and compare it with the **fixed** `reconciliation.sourceFingerprint` the administrator reviewed; if they differ, throw `STALE_SOURCE` (a new fingerprint is never accepted silently, on any attempt or retry); only then build the source-year payload from the transaction's fresh read plus the appended records and events (never from in-memory state) and `tx.set` both documents. The transaction still runs inside `this._saveQueue.enqueue(...)`, behind any pending save of the active year, and a failure is captured and re-thrown after the queue settles exactly as today. The legacy path keeps `writeBatch`.
- **The callback is free of external side effects**: it mutates no service state, emits no notifications or toasts, allocates no ids (every id is computed before the transaction from the draft), and performs no reads or writes other than its two `get`s and two `set`s, because the SDK may run the callback more than once under contention. State, the draft, and the UI change only after the returned promise settles.
- **Rules and permissions.** `firestore.rules` grants `allow read, write` on `users/{userId}/billingYears/{yearId}` on the owner condition alone, with no field, key, or diff constraints, so the transaction's reads (including a `get` of the absent destination) and its two `set`s are permitted for the signed-in owner with no rules change. The implementation ticket re-verifies this against `tests/rules/firestore.rules.test.js` with a transaction-shaped test (get absent destination, get source, set both) before claiming it.
- `computeSourceFingerprint(state)` is a pure, key-order-independent hash over `status`, `bills` (id, name, amount, billingFrequency, members), `familyMembers` (id, name, linkedMembers), `payments` (id, memberId, amount, type, reversed), `creditAdjustments`, `owedAdjustments`, and `billingEvents.length`. Settings and template fields are excluded. It is computed once when Step 4 is shown and stored on the draft as the reviewed fingerprint; refreshing the preview replaces it only after the administrator sees the new totals.
- **Outcomes.** `NEXT_YEAR_EXISTS` — the destination exists; the service reads its `billingEvents`: a `YEAR_CREATED_FROM_REVIEW` event carrying this draft's `reconciliationId` means the operation already completed (switch to it, clear the draft, no second commit); otherwise it was created elsewhere (keep the draft, show the created-elsewhere state, never commit). `STALE_SOURCE` — nothing written; the page refreshes the preview from the current state, shows what changed, and requires Step 4 to be confirmed again before Start is enabled. `COMMIT_FAILED` — a definite failure before or at commit (`aborted` after the SDK's own retries, `failed-precondition`, `permission-denied`, `invalid-argument`, a thrown validation error): nothing written, the draft kept, Retry offered. `COMMIT_UNCERTAIN` — `unavailable`, `deadline-exceeded`, `unknown`, `internal`, a cancelled request, or a non-Firestore error after the commit was sent: the service reads the destination and the source year's events back before any retry and classifies the result as completed (clear the draft, switch), created elsewhere (keep the draft, stop), or not committed (Retry allowed with the same `reconciliationId`, ids, and reviewed fingerprint).
- **Offline.** A Firestore transaction cannot run offline (`tx.get` rejects with `unavailable`, "client is offline"); the page treats it as `COMMIT_UNCERTAIN` only when the commit was sent, otherwise as `COMMIT_FAILED` with the message "You're offline. Nothing was saved; your draft is kept." Retry re-runs the guard and the transaction; it never falls back to a batch or to a write without the preconditions.
- Repeated attempts therefore produce one logically identical rollover: deterministic ids mean a completed commit is recognizable, the destination precondition means it is never overwritten, and the fingerprint means source records are never appended twice.
- **Tests** (`tests/react/lib/BillingYearService.test.js`, with a `runTransaction` mock that can replay the callback, fail the commit, or reject after committing): a stale preview (a payment recorded after the preview → `STALE_SOURCE`, nothing written); two sessions creating the same label (the second gets `NEXT_YEAR_EXISTS` and does not overwrite); a retry after `COMMIT_FAILED` producing exactly the same records and ids; a successful commit whose response is lost (`unavailable` after commit → read-back finds `YEAR_CREATED_FROM_REVIEW` with the reconciliation id → completed, no second commit); the callback replayed by the SDK producing no duplicate records or side effects; a definite failure leaving no destination document and no source change; and the legacy call still using `writeBatch`.

### Worked examples (test fixtures)

The wireframe sample year is 2026 (`plans/ffb-wireframes.html`: nine members in five households, five bills). Fixtures 1, 2, 3, and 6 map onto it; 4, 5, and 7 are unit fixtures with invented bills, as noted.

1. **Price increase (Northline Mobile, wireframe bill `nl`).** $180 → $198/mo from July, nine members. `(198 − 180) × 6 = $108.00`; `splitCents(10800, 9)` = $12.00 each. Nine True-up records in 2026, `basis: 'price_change'`, born `carried_forward`; 2026 settlement metrics identical before and after Start; 2027 `carry_opening` items include +$12.00 per Northline member at the household grain; 2027 Northline is $198/mo with a `BILL_PRICE_ROLLED_OVER` event.
2. **Annual increase and rounding remainder (invented $139/yr bill, three members).** $139 → $159/yr from March: `((159 − 139) / 12) × 10 = 16.666…` → `toCents` = 1667 → $16.67, **not** $16.70. `splitCents(1667, 3)` = $5.55, $5.55, $5.57 (sum $16.67; the last member absorbs the remainder). The wireframe's analogue is Harbor Cloud Storage $120 → $144/yr from March across four members: $20.00, $5.00 each.
3. **Late start (invented $9.99/mo subscription, three members).** From September: `9.99 × 4 = $39.96`; `splitCents(3996, 3)` = $13.32 each. Absent from 2026 `bills[]`; present in 2027 at $9.99/mo with the same three members and a `BILL_CREATED` event with `source: 'year_end_review'`. The wireframe names it Lumen Fiber Backup (Kenji, Dede, Sam).
4. **Price decrease (invented $30/mo bill, three fully paid members).** $30 → $24/mo from October: `(24 − 30) × 3 = −$18.00` → a Service Credit of $18.00, $6.00 per member, `basis: 'price_change'`, with `calc`. 2027 uses $24/mo. Permitted from an Open, Settling, or Closed source year (ADR 0009); from a Closed year the resulting $6.00 Credits are carried in the same commit.
5. **Ended and net credit (invented $12/mo bill, three members in one fully paid household).** Last month August: `12 × 4 = $48.00`, $16.00 per member, `basis: 'ended'`. With no other balance or charges the household's Credit carries as one −$48.00 item whose `detail[]` lists the three −$16.00 Service Credits (non-additive); the bill is absent from 2027. Same status rule as fixture 4.
6. **Usage Charge (wireframe: Maja Lindqvist, a linked member).** A $30.00 activation fee already recorded as deferred on a linked member is prefilled in Step 3 and carried as a +$30.00 item attributed to Maja under the Lindqvist household; it is not re-recorded.
7. **Netting (unit fixture: a household with two Northline members, a $22.00 Credit, and one $12.00 True-up each).** Top-level items −$22.00, +$12.00, +$12.00; one seed of +$2.00. Any Service Credit detail beneath the Credit is non-additive. The wireframe's Okafor household is the three-member variant: −$26.00 (a $22.00 overpayment plus a −$4.00 ended-bill credit for Ada), +$12.00 × 3, +$5.00 → one seed of +$15.00.

8. **Already credited (unit fixture).** A $12/mo bill with three members ended after August, where a $48.00 Service Credit was already recorded on it from the Bills tab in September: the Ended row shows "Already credited: $48.00", the credit to record defaults to $0.00, nothing is recorded, and the bill is absent from 2027; owed is reduced once.
9. **Excess credit (unit fixture).** See § Itemized carry, fixture 9 there: −$132.00 preserved across a year with no bills.
