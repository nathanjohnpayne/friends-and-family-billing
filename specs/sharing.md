# Sharing

Covers share link generation, token validation, the share link dialog, and the public-facing share view for family members.

## Approved money projection (#629)

[ADR 0010](../docs/adr/0010-household-cent-projection.md#public-compatibility-and-release-boundary) owns exact component/row authority and the approved #636 Decisions A–C: public `T = I + B`, `I = max(0, G − S + O)`, `N = P − R`, balance `T − N`, active-report context and versioned available/unavailable payloads. This is a contract for later implementation, not current runtime conformance. Household totals never sum rounded per-member displays (#620). Both writers and the actual cache/Function consumers activate together after the [expanded M4 checkpoint](../plans/629-cent-rounding-rollout.md#m4-both-public-writers-and-money-version-consumer-after-m3-and-638); Functions-first, Hosting-second and complete-set release gates remain.

Under the [owner's source-boundary decision](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/633#issuecomment-6690912942), every admitted source money field must be an actual finite JavaScript Number (`typeof value === 'number' && Number.isFinite(value)`). Numeric strings, `NaN`, infinities, `null`, `undefined` and booleans cause an integrity hold for the affected household only; do not publish coerced or partial money totals for it. Internal hold evidence names household, record id, field and observed type. Keep existing presence signals and scope/privacy rules; this decision does not authorize exposing private hold evidence in public payloads. The public hold rendering and response contract are defined under Unavailable summary below; the private adapter diagnostics are not that payload. Source recorders keep existing write-time parsing, corrections use the app to write Numbers, and no stored conversion or migration is authorized. The [rollout inventory and gates](../plans/629-cent-rounding-rollout.md#source-sign-and-domain-guard-inventory) govern sign/domain exclusions, removal of coerced-money tests during M2b, and M5's non-conforming-field hold counts by component and record ids without authorizing a live read.

## Acceptance Criteria

### Share Helpers

- `buildShareScopes` always includes `summary:read`, `paymentMethods:read`, `usageCharges:read` (#317 — a member always sees their own pending charges on their share, so the scope is on every normal link), and `payments:read` (#356 — a member's payment history is their own data, the same posture as `usageCharges:read`); adds `disputes:create` and/or `disputes:read` when the respective flags are true; and adds `refunds:read` when the third flag is true (#319). The 2-argument dispute call stays backwards-compatible. Links minted before `payments:read` existed simply won't carry it, so the share page degrades gracefully (omits payment history).
- All public creation and rebuild paths supply one complete raw snapshot (`familyMembers`, `bills`, `payments`, `creditAdjustments`, `owedAdjustments`), year status and complete household Refund Notice context, explicitly ready and bound to the same owner/year. This includes ShareLinkService, ShareLink/Email/Text dialogs, InvoicingTab's direct preview writer, RefundNoticeService/ChargeNoticeService and household contact/name rebuilds in BillingYearService. Loading/failed/absent context yields unavailable, never an empty/null fallback to finish a write. Both writers reuse the board's existing `reopenedCreditAdjustmentIds` helper for open/settling years, and null for closed/archived years; no public eligibility mirror. The Function reads household notices internally even without `refunds:read`, but that grants no disclosure of notice records, ids or fields.
- Both public writers use one `projectHouseholdMoney` result for I/B/P/R/N/T/balance and allocated rows, with identical cents for the same snapshot. `combinedAnnualTotal` stays I, and its monthly presentation excludes B/12. Add household **Usage charges billed** B and **Total owed** T after the floored I reconciliation; deferred Usage Charges stay outside T and per-token-member. **Paid to date** / `totalPaid` is N, explained by **Payments received** P and one signed **Refunds / credit carried forward** R deduction; zero B/R rows are omitted, nonzero rows never omitted or independently rounded. R is never subtracted on the bill side. No new refund entitlement or U decision follows.
- `buildPendingChargesForShare` returns the token member's OWN deferred Usage Charges (per-member grain, ADR 0005 — "a linked member sees their own pending charges"; the household grain is only for the admin settlement board) as `{ charges, total, count }`: only `kind: 'usage_charge'` records with `status: 'deferred'` whose `memberId` is the token member are included; voided/billed charges and other members' charges (including linked household members) are excluded; charges are sorted by incurred date ascending and each carries a `runningTotal`; only member-safe fields (`id`, `description`, `amount`, `incurredDate`, `runningTotal`) are exposed; an unknown member or empty input yields an empty result.
- `buildPaymentHistoryForShare` (#356) returns the household's (the token member plus their linked members — the same grain as `totalPaid`) settled payments as member-safe line items: `{ payments: [{ id, date, amount, method }], count }`, newest first. Only "live" ledger entries are projected — reversal entries (`type: 'reversal'`) and reversed originals (`reversed === true`) are excluded, so their allocated cents sum to the projected household `P` (exact original/reversal pairs net to zero under ADR 0010). Only member-safe fields are exposed (`id`, `date` = the payment's `receivedAt`, `amount`, `method`); the free-text `note` is never included. `buildPublicShareData` includes `paymentHistory` in the generated `publicShares` document when the scopes carry `payments:read`, and the Cloud Function `resolveShareToken` mirrors the same field (via the `functions/billing.js` mirror) into both its response and the self-healed doc, so the cache-hit, cache-miss, and self-healed doc all agree (an explicit cache↔CF parity test asserts the two builders produce identical output). The history allocates projected `P` to eligible live rows under ADR 0010; it does not independently recompute financial authority or change stored ledger values.
- `buildCarriedItemsForShare` (ADR 0009, `specs/year-end-review.md` § Member-facing surfaces) returns the household's itemized opening balance as `{ fromYear, notApplied, remainingCredit, items: [{ memberId, memberName, kind, basis, billName, description, amount, detail }], total }` from the primary's and linked members' `carry_opening` seeds: one signed line per carried True-up share, member charge, or household credit (the credit's `detail[]` is explanatory, never added), `total` equal to projected net opening `O` (ADR 0010), only member-safe fields, nothing from other households. A seed without `items` yields one line `{ kind: 'legacy', description: 'Carried from <year>', amount }`. `buildPublicShareData` includes it as `carriedItems` under `summary:read` when the total is non-zero or any item exists, and the Cloud Function `resolveShareToken` mirrors the same field through `functions/billing.js` into both its response and the self-healed doc (reconciled with `FieldValue.delete()` when absent, like `serviceCredits`), with an explicit cache↔CF parity test; the function ships before the client renders the section. Two numbers are kept apart: `notApplied = max(0, −(billsSubtotal − serviceCreditTotal + total))`, the household's floor remainder shown by the "Not applied this year" row (it includes any Service Credit excess, which the rollover forfeits), and `remainingCredit`, the unused incoming credit the rollover carries and the only amount the "$Y remains" sentence states, computed from the net `total` exactly as the rollover computes `U`: `I = max(0, −total)`, `Q = max(0, total)`, `A = max(0, billsSubtotal − serviceCreditTotal + Q)`, `remainingCredit = max(0, I − A)`; the seed's charge/credit split never enters it, so a +$10.00 charge and −$10.00 credit under a Service Credit excess show nothing remaining (`specs/year-end-review.md` § Member-facing surfaces and § Itemized carry define both the same way; fixtures 10l and 10n).
- `buildServiceCreditsForShare` returns the household's active Service Credits (#337) as member-safe line items for the share summary: `{ items: [{ reason, billName, amount }], total }`. Active `service_credit` records for the primary plus linked members are aggregated by (billId + reason) so a bill-level split (one record per affected member) shows as a single line; the `+owed` Usage Charge direction, voided credits, `carry_opening` seeds, and other members' credits are excluded; only member-safe fields are exposed. The `total` is projected `S`, already folded into `combinedAnnualTotal`; group rows receive ADR 0010 largest-remainder cents by the deterministic constituent-record identity, so two $0.005 groups total $0.01, not $0.02. `buildPublicShareData` includes `serviceCredits` in the generated `publicShares` document under `summary:read` when the total is positive, and the Cloud Function `resolveShareToken` mirrors the same field (via the `functions/billing.js` mirror) so the cache-hit, cache-miss, and self-healed doc all agree.
- `buildShareTokenDoc` includes rawToken when truthy and omits it when null (invoice flow); sets defaults for revoked (false), lastAccessedAt (null), accessCount (0).
- `buildShareUrl` constructs a URL in the format `{origin}/share.html?token={token}`.
- `computeExpiryDate` returns null for 0, null, or negative days; returns a future Date for positive days.
- `isShareTokenStale` returns true for revoked tokens, true for expired tokens, false for non-expired non-revoked tokens, false when no expiry is set, and correctly handles Firestore Timestamp-like objects with `.toDate()`.

### ShareView (Public-Facing)

- Shows "No share token provided" error when no token is in the URL.
- After #676 every upgraded visit POSTs to `/resolveShareToken`; ShareView never directly reads publicShares or renders a raw cache. The admission response alone supplies identity/year and available/unavailable sections, with version/state/shape validation before rendering. Server admission may reuse only matching certified **available** money within one hour when the complete canonical source inputs are unchanged; a certified unavailable state always source-projects on every admission regardless of age/guard. Changed canonical source inputs, invalid certification/envelope mismatch or age over one hour force one source projection and serving of that fresh result, available or unavailable; no legacy flash or stale fallback.
- Missing/incompatible/replaced server caches are resolved within admission. An incompatible/malformed admission response or failed lookup shows a recoverable load failure and no cached amount; valid-token unavailable remains distinct from that failure and invalid/expired/revoked links.
- Invalid/expired/revoked links show the server error or existing invalid-link fallback; lookup/compatibility failures remain recoverable load failures, distinct from valid-token unavailable.
- Current behavior until #676 lands: on publicShares cache hit, renders member name heading, year pill, and bumps access count via updateDoc. A `summary:read` cache without `carriedItems` resolves through the already-deployed Cloud Function before rendering, even when its timestamp is fresh: the old cache shape cannot distinguish no carry from incoming credit hidden by the owed floor. A failed resolution follows the normal error path without rendering the old summary. Until a future shared schema version distinguishes these shapes, uncarried summaries resolve once per visit; links without summary scope and itemized-carry caches retain the cache path.
- On cache miss with successful Cloud Function response: renders data from the response and sends the correct POST payload to `/resolveShareToken`.
- On cache miss with failed response: shows the server error message or a generic "invalid or has been removed" fallback.
- On network/unexpected error: shows "Could not connect" message.
- Bills section renders bill names, monthly amounts, annual shares, split counts, member count labels, and a TOTAL row. Money sections and bill-dependent creation below render only while financialState is available.
- "Request Review" buttons appear when scopes include `disputes:create`; hidden when they do not.
- Dispute form validates empty messages (shows error styling), clears error on typing, calls `addDoc` with correct fields (memberId, memberName, billId, billName, message, proposedCorrection, status, createdAt, tokenHash) on submit, and shows a success message.
- Cancel button closes the dispute form overlay.
- Payment summary shows I, its monthly presentation, **Usage charges billed** B, **Total owed** T, net **Paid to date** N, the P/R reconciliation and balance T−N. Progress and callouts use N/T, with no percentage or division when T=0. Preserve existing signed-balance credit semantics and no new refund entitlement.
- Evidence viewing: opens `window.open` directly when evidence has a downloadUrl; fetches via `/getEvidenceUrl` when no downloadUrl, showing "Loading..." while fetching.
- Dispute approval/rejection: shows Approve/Reject buttons when `userReview.state` is "requested"; clicking Approve calls updateDoc and shows "You approved this resolution."; hides buttons when state is "approved_by_user" or "rejected_by_user".
- Payment methods section renders method labels, handles, and Copy buttons; hidden when empty.
- Payment history section (#357) renders the household's settled payments from the `payments:read` payload (`paymentHistory`, built by `buildPaymentHistoryForShare`) behind a collapsed "Show payment history ({count})" entry point that expands to a date / method / amount list (method via `getPaymentMethodLabel`, amount via `formatCurrency`). Only member-safe fields are shown — never the free-text note. The section degrades gracefully: it is omitted entirely when the resolved data carries no `paymentHistory` (older links that predate `payments:read`) or an empty list.
- Disputes section renders dispute details (bill name, status, message, resolution note); hidden when empty. Existing Review Request status and reply/approve/reject workflow remain available under `disputes:read` during a financial hold, independently of money; bill-dependent Question This creation stays withheld.
- Pending charges (deferred Usage Charges, #317) render as a compact amber **notice** (mockup redesign) placed right after the bill/summary (not at the bottom of the page): a "{count} upcoming charge(s) — not yet due" header, one line per charge (amount · description · incurred date), and a total — only when the resolved share data carries a non-empty `pendingCharges` (which the Cloud Function returns only for the `usageCharges:read` scope); hidden when the scope is absent or there are no deferred charges. The notice states both dispositions of a pending charge — it will either be billed separately by the admin (the off-cycle Charge Notice path, #320) or applied to the member's bill next year (the carry-forward seam, #322) (#338).
- **Carried from &lt;year&gt;** (ADR 0009): when the resolved data carries `carriedItems`, the page renders the itemized breakdown headed "Carried from 2026" directly under the household total reconciliation, as an expandable panel that is open by default and is toggled by the reconciliation row's amount and by the lead sentence's "carried from &lt;year&gt;" link: lines grouped by member (a linked member's lines under that member), the household credit with its detail lines at the household level, and a "Carried total" line; the household total reconciliation shows the carried total as its own line, and "Not applied this year" holds the `max(0, …)` floor remainder, if any, so the opening balance is never shown twice. A legacy-only seed shows one signed Carried row without an itemized panel. Copy follows `specs/year-end-review.md` § Member-facing copy and never says True-up, carry_opening, or adjustment. A legacy seed renders one "Carried from 2026: +$30.00" line. Hidden when there are no carried items.
- Service credits reconciliation (#337): when the resolved data carries `serviceCredits`, the Household's Annual Billing Summary renders a reconciliation under the household total — the bills subtotal, each Service Credit as a `-$amount` line (`Service credit — {billName} ({reason})`), a **Carried from &lt;year&gt;** line for the opening balance (signed; with `carriedItems` present the amount toggles the itemized breakdown rendered directly beneath the reconciliation, open by default; a legacy seed shows the one line), a **Not applied this year** line for the floor remainder when the credits and the carried credit exceed the bills (positive), never the label "Other adjustments" (ADR 0009 member-facing terminology), and the resulting floored annual-bills I; the nonzero household **Usage charges billed** B row then leads to **Total owed** T — so the reduced annual total is explained rather than silently lower than the sum of the bills. Service-credit detail is hidden when there are no active Service Credits; a nonzero B row still renders without them.
- Trust banner renders a "secure annual billing summary" notice.
- Shows loading message initially while data is being fetched.

### Unavailable summary (#636 Decision C)

- Both writers emit `moneyProjectionVersion: 1` and `financialState: 'available' | 'unavailable'`, following ADR 0010's scoped payload contract. A valid-token unavailable result retains identity/year/scope and independently authorized historical sections, without private holds/causes/source ids/raw values/provenance/tie keys or expanded public ids.
- Render exactly **Financial data unavailable**, **We can’t show current billing amounts. Please contact the administrator.** and **Try again**. Omit summary/bill money, paymentSummary, Service Credits, carried items, payment-history money and the entire pending-charges section, including counts. Withhold pay links/QR, percentage/progress, Settled/Outstanding/Credit claims and bill-dependent Question This entry points. No zero/partial/stale summary substitutes.
- The unavailable block replaces money sections only; no whole-page early return. Existing scoped Refund Notices with frozen amounts and receipt/not-received actions, and Review Request status/reply, render from their own payload keys. Missing historical amounts render absent.
- Try again re-reads source, normalizes nothing, changes no year document and retries no money write; existing self-healing/cache/metric writes remain. Held → retry → held stays unavailable; once a view observes a hold, only a compatible authoritative available response restores monetary rendering. No stale fallback after unavailable or incompatible authoritative response.
- Under [approved Decision #671](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/671#issuecomment-6758892501), every upgraded visit is admitted transactionally. After #676 the client never raw-reads publicShares, renders only admission responses and removes `refreshOnly`/separate counting. Private certification older than one hour recomputes in the same request, with no stale/background response. Targeted transitions force projection regardless of age. Compatibility is not freshness: exact parity applies to one source snapshot, not different read times. A marker cannot retrofit old clients; the Functions-first/old-client interval remains a separate release gate.
- Merge-based self-healing and scope removal delete stale optional monetary keys, rather than just attaching version/state to old fields. Preserve real token expiry/revocation, counters and live-only detailed notices.
- Required tests run through real handler/client/cache paths: held → retry → held → corrected; incompatible Function version/shape; incompatible/missing/malformed cache versions/states/shapes; late old-cache replacement after self-healing; aged certification recomputed in the same transaction; young unavailable certification always reprojected, including a changed-source ordinary correction followed by a new visit without Try again; held household receipt/non-receipt persistence and open Review reply; historical section alongside unavailable; no pay/QR/Question This/percentage/settlement claims/pending section while held. Arithmetic/reopening vectors are in ADR 0010's shared matrix.

### Refund Notices on the Share Page (#319)

- When the token carries `refunds:read` and the resolved data includes `refundNotices`, a "Your Refunds" section renders each notice with its amount, payment method, and reason; the section is hidden when there are no notices. This historical section and its actions render independently during an unavailable summary, reading only frozen stored amount/method/reason/status/confirmation; never recompute from held money or invent a missing amount.
- A pending notice (no `confirmation`) shows two actions: **Confirm Receipt** and **I Have Not Received It**. Clicking either POSTs `{ token, noticeId, outcome }` to `/submitRefundConfirmation` (`outcome` is `confirm` or `not_received`)—never a direct Firestore write.
- After confirming, the card shows "You confirmed you received this refund." and the action buttons are removed; after reporting non-receipt, it shows the "reported … as not received" message. A notice that already carries a `confirmation` seeds that terminal state on load (no action buttons).
- If the `/submitRefundConfirmation` POST fails, the card surfaces the server (or a generic) error message and leaves the action buttons in place so the member can retry; the error clears on the next attempt.
- **Live resolution:** because Refund Notices are dynamic (mutable confirmation state) and are NOT stored in the `publicShares` cache — and a freshly issued confirm link is minted before its notice document exists — a `refunds:read` link always resolves live via `resolveShareToken` even on a cache hit. Only dedicated refund-confirm links carry `refunds:read`; ordinary upgraded visits may reuse certified money only through server admission. Internal notice reads for R do not change this disclosure boundary.
- **Public balance reopening (Decision B):** in open/settling years an unresolved `not_received` excludes its linked disposition from public R, like the board; confirmed/unreported/resolved notices use normal eligibility and cancelled dispositions remain excluded. Closed/archived years and ordinary invoices use null context. Year status is read with the projection snapshot; late reports cannot reopen closed-year money.
- [Decision #671](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/671#issuecomment-6758892501) supersedes B condition 4 with logical admission invalidation: the first upgraded admission after report, resolution, disposition cancellation or close projects fresh money. Handlers need no cache knowledge. Reports made while held/read-only still change the private guard. Required real-component sequence within one hour: hold → report → admission recertifies unavailable with reported history → ordinary invalid-amount correction → new visit without Try again projects available/reopened money; successful report starts an authoritative reread and rejects deliberately delayed earlier responses. No stale fallback restores money.
- The [consistency plan for the approved architecture](../plans/636-public-cache-consistency.md) records private certificate/digest/age/transaction/metrics/rules-proof requirements and [corrected condition 6](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/671#issuecomment-6759162183): client caches keep version 1, carry no certificate of their own, and may serve only content whose complete monetary digest matches the private certificate under valid age and unchanged complete canonical source inputs. Identical rewrites reuse certified available content only while actual source inputs remain unchanged without a new certificate or private age reset; mismatching or incompatible content forces source projection. No persistence exception or replacement binding is needed. Changed inputs call the canonical authority once and publish/serve its fresh available/unavailable result with a new certificate/private age anchor, never old valid money after validation or a memo-only update. Unchanged available inputs avoid projection/certificate rewrite but still write the visit increment. The [current-integrity owner decision](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/671#issuecomment-6771296181) and plan require all seven real-path fixtures; validation input includes five raw arrays, financial/eligibility settings, year status, household membership and canonical notice reopening/cancellation context, while payment-method-only presentation settings remain outside it. Unsafe fingerprinting forces canonical projection and fail-closed admission rather than a partial digest; repeat #675's M audit for the complete fingerprint/all hold classes. Payment-method/metrics-only sync preserves certified age/count semantics and never restores held payment/QR controls. Every committed admission increments once; retries within that transaction add none. Architecture approval does not authorize this head or source work. #492 raw public-document access remains separate and unfixed.
- **Routing:** `/submitRefundConfirmation` is registered as a Hosting rewrite in `firebase.json` (before the SPA catch-all), alongside the other share Cloud Functions, so the member POST reaches the function in deployed Hosting.
- `resolveShareToken`'s `disputes:read` projection excludes `kind: 'refund_notice'` documents (mirroring `useDisputes`), so a normal review-enabled link never renders Refund Notices as empty Review Requests (ADR 0002).

### RefundNoticeService (issuance)

- `issueRefundNotice` mints a `refunds:read` share link (also granting `summary:read`) for the confirm CTA, writes a `refund_notice` doc into the `disputes` subcollection with a server `createdAt`, the snapshot fields, a null `confirmation`, the `creditAdjustmentId`, and the link's `tokenHash`, then emails the member the reason, amount, method, and confirm link.
- The notice persists even when the member has no email (no email sent), when the email send fails (best-effort), or when minting the share link fails (the notice is written without a `tokenHash` and the email goes out without the share URL). Returns the new `noticeId` and `shareUrl`.
