# Exact household components and integer-cent financial authority

Accepted 2026-11-05 for [#629](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/629). The [approved proposal](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/629#issuecomment-6675576490) and [owner's four conditions](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/629#issuecomment-6675648119) authorize this bounded amendment before implementation. This ADR is the single rounding/allocation definition; [the rollout plan](../../plans/629-cent-rounding-rollout.md) separates implementation and release gates. Acceptance of the contract is not approval of any code head or production operation.

## Boundary and exact inputs

Stored bills, payments and reversals, adjustments, seeds and audit history retain their values, including legacy sub-cent values. There is no normalization, migration or backfill. Preserve annualization, bill/member splitting, household membership, eligibility, statuses, cancellation, and reopened-refund rules. In particular, retain the current bill denominator and treatment of assigned member ids; this amendment does not repair unrelated data or change membership rules.

The [owner's source-boundary decision](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/633#issuecomment-6690912942) ([supporting record](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/633#issuecomment-6690891415)) requires every money field on a record admitted by the existing eligibility predicates to be an actual finite JavaScript Number: `typeof value === 'number' && Number.isFinite(value)`. This applies to all six components, including bill amounts and payments, and to admitted deferred-charge and seed/item/detail money fields. Numeric strings, `NaN`, infinities, `null`, `undefined` and booleans are non-conforming; do not parse, coerce, omit or replace them with zero. A non-conforming field puts the affected household on an integrity hold naming the household, record id, field and observed type. As with infeasible allocation, the hold blocks settlement, caps, close, carry and public money projection for that household only. M3 uses its private unavailable contract; the public M4 contract is defined below under the owner's Decisions A–C, before source work.

Eligibility and record presence remain separate from source validity. A non-conforming record still exists for presence signals such as the recorded-refund warning. Write-time parsing in recorders is unchanged; the owner corrects a record through the app, which writes a Number. This is no migration, stored conversion or normalization. M2b (#634), when it moves S/B summation onto this authority, replaces the two deliberate leading-numeric-string `parseFloat` test locks with household-hold assertions; no test may assert coerced money after that activation, and legacy display helpers must not decide money once M2 is active. M2a (#633) remains unused and does not change those active callers or locks early.

The first relevant PR body inventories sign/domain guards, including non-positive Service Credits and payment reversals. A silent drop defaults to an integrity hold unless documented product behavior justifies the exclusion; an existing implementation guard alone is not that justification. M1/#632 (merged as #639) contains generic primitives and no source-domain eligibility guards; M2a/#633 owns the detailed domain inventory in the [rollout plan](../../plans/629-cent-rounding-rollout.md#source-sign-and-domain-guard-inventory). No other financial or product policy is changed by this source-type decision.

Interpret each admitted finite numeric **source field** by its decimal `Number.toString()` representation (including exponent notation); carry exact decimal coefficients/rational denominators through multiplication, division and aggregation. For bills, annualize the stored price and divide by assigned-member count exactly, then sum the household's eligible member shares. Never reconstruct exact inputs from an already accumulated floating-point summary, monthly display value, or rounded member line. Raw calculations remain available for audit/comparison.

Define `q(x)` as exact dollars → integer cents, nearest cent with exact half-cent ties away from zero; normalize signed zero to integer `0`. No binary epsilon is added. All six components aggregate their exact eligible raw rows **before one quantization per household/component**:

| Cents | Exact source aggregate |
| --- | --- |
| `G` | Gross annual bill shares for the household |
| `S` | Active Service Credits for its members |
| `O` | Net signed opening balance from eligible `carry_opening` seed **amounts**, never their detail |
| `P` | Gross payments including original and reversal entries |
| `R` | Effective recorded credit dispositions under the caller's existing reopened-refund eligibility |
| `B` | Billed Usage Charges |

Compose only integer cents: `N = P − R`; `owed = max(0, G − S + O) + B`; `balance = owed − N`; `credit = max(0, N − owed)`. These cents govern household displays, existing financial gates/caps, settlement metrics, close gates and carry. Preserve overpayment eligibility: `recordPayment` and `SettlementBoard.handleRecordPayment` require a positive amount but have no amount ceiling; this amendment introduces no payment cap. Payment-button availability and the existing refund caps consume the projected authority. A one-cent shortfall is outstanding; zero shortfall is settled subject to the existing Credit/Partial/status classification. A cap comparison uses integer cents, with no tolerance, and recomputes the affected component from the **proposed post-mutation raw ledger snapshot** under the existing cap rule. Never validate only `q(request)` or add independently rounded requests to an old component: retained sub-cent records can cross a cent boundary in aggregate (old `R = $0.012`, proposed refund `$0.004`: `q(request) = 0` but projected `R` rises from 1 to 2 cents). Preserve existing request validation and append-only writes; this is not input normalization. Deferred Usage Charges remain outside current owed. Ordinary invoice scope remains `I = max(0, G − S + O)`, with B excluded. The public share uses `T = I + B` and balance `T − N`; I is floored before B is added. The selected Charge Notice remains its own allocation parent, not a second authority for household B.

The [owner's invoice decision for #635](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/635#issuecomment-6714971444) specifies the payment side of ordinary invoices: `I = max(0, G − S + O)`, `N = P − R`, and invoice balance `I − N`, with the default **null** refund-reopening context. `B` remains excluded; never reuse settlement balance here. Payment Received remains `P`; a single **Refunded** reconciliation row appears only when `R > 0`, so `I − P + R` equals the invoice balance. With `R = 0`, existing copy and negative-balance behavior remain unchanged. This changes no refund entitlement, cap, eligibility or carry-only `U` decision; #617 stays open. [Decision B for #636](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/636#issuecomment-6755898275) explicitly limits condition 2 of that invoice decision to ordinary invoices; the original comment is preserved. Public writers adopt N with the distinct reopening context below and remain a separate implementation/release gate.

The [owner's selected Charge Notice decision for #635](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/635#issuecomment-6714986894) declares each selected Notice as its **own allocation parent**: `T = q(sum of exact raw selected charge amounts)`. Use the allocator below with ascending ordinal `String(record.id)` ties; running totals sum allocated cents. One projection serves preview and **new** presentation snapshots, including their outgoing email. Historical Notice snapshots render as stored; neither they nor source charge amounts are rewritten. A zero-cent `T` is not issuable; invalid selected money holds the household and blocks preview/issue/send/copy before any side effect. There is no promise that separately issued Notices sum to household `B`: only `B` decides owed. A difference requires sub-cent source charges; both existing charge writers persist whole cents and receive tripwire tests. The complete selected-scope fixtures and private unavailable contract are in [the invoicing spec](../../specs/invoicing.md#charge-notice-projection-and-new-snapshots).

The previous promise to leave `calculations.js` unchanged is superseded **only** for this projection boundary and its consumers. `calculateAnnualSummary` may retain raw audit/member-display calculations; its raw accumulated output is not the exact component input. No annualization, eligibility or owed formula change beyond the authorized quantization boundary is permitted.

## Incoming credit, carry and epsilon retirement

In integer cents define `I = max(0, −O)`, `Q = max(0, O)`, `A = max(0, G − S + Q)`, and `U = max(0, I − A)`. `U` stays carry-only: it never enters `N`, never creates a new refund entitlement, and receives no `carry_forward` disposition. Service Credit excess is not money. Net opening is authoritative: +$10 and −$10 seeds net to zero before this calculation. #617 remains open.

Carry's separately eligible deferred-charge aggregate is also exact and quantized once to cents; it is not a seventh current-owed component. Its projected rows conserve that target. The new seed cents equal deferred-charge cents minus payment-backed credit cents minus `U`; new seed/item/detail values are written as cents divided by 100 under the existing record model. Stored True-up and Service Credit amounts still use **`splitCents`**, which gives every member except the last `floor(totalCents/n)` and gives the last member the remainder. Do not replace that stored allocation with the display allocation below.

All carry thresholds become exact comparisons (`U > 0`, credit/deferred cents `> 0`, zero net `== 0`). Invariant (a) becomes exact integer-cent equality: each projected seed's signed item cents sum to its seed cents; its non-additive credit detail cents sum to the credit-item cents. Conservation and old-year settlement invariants also compare integer cents exactly. A zero-net review seed may retain nonzero offsetting items as before. A chain without new bills/payments conserves the same carried cents. Reopened refunds remain excluded from carry under its existing null-reopened-set rule, and remain visible in source-year settlement under its active reopened set.

`CREDIT_EPSILON` has **no residual role in projected financial decisions, caps, settlement, close, carry, allocation or these invariants**. During implementation it may remain only in explicitly legacy/raw audit-comparison code that cannot feed those outcomes; remove the constant when no such callers remain. It is not an integrity tolerance or a display exception. This retires ADR 0009's former epsilon invariant and carry threshold.

## Projected rows: largest remainder, never stored reallocation

Only rows that promise to sum to a projected component/parent use this allocation. For exact signed row values in cents `x_i` and integer parent target `T`, start each row at `floor(x_i)` (also for negative rows). Before awarding cents, prove feasibility with exact rational/integer arithmetic: `k = T − Σ floor(x_i)` must satisfy `0 ≤ k ≤ Σ (ceil(x_i) − floor(x_i)) ≤ n`. Equivalently, `Σ floor(x_i) ≤ T ≤ Σ ceil(x_i)`. Then award one increment to each of the first `k` rows with a positive fractional remainder, in descending fractional-remainder order; no row receives more than one increment or leaves its floor/ceiling bounds. An exact integer row retains its exact cents; in particular an exact zero row stays zero. Equal fractions use **ascending ordinal lexicographic `String(record.id)`**, with case preserved, no locale collation, numeric sorting, date or input order. Allocation changes projection values only; raw source amounts stay inspectable in administrator audit paths and are not added to public payloads.

Keep existing display group membership: for example, Service Credit rows still group by existing bill-id/reason rules; this does not infer an earlier record's business purpose or alter per-record review netting. A group's deterministic identity is the JSON encoding of its sorted constituent record-id strings; its tie-break key is its lexicographically smallest constituent record id. Disjoint groups have distinct keys. Bill rows use `String(bill.id)` and aggregate the household's exact shares for that bill. Allocations within a group use its constituent record ids. Missing/duplicate ambiguous ids or inconsistent provenance cause an integrity hold; never fall back to array order or invent a balancing record. Sorting a projection does not reorder a persisted member list for `splitCents`.

Preserve exact original/reversal pairs **before** allocating other `P` rows: validate the existing `reversesPaymentId` link and exact equal-and-opposite raw amounts; project the original by `q`, its reversal as its negation, so the pair contributes zero. Allocate the remaining payment rows to the remaining `P` target. The public history continues to omit reversed originals and reversal entries; an administrator history that shows both uses the opposite projected values. A malformed/unmatched claimed reversal is an integrity hold, not a silently dropped or independently rounded payment. This does not delete or modify ledger records.

For opening balance, validate provenance and parent/child conservation at the canonical cent boundary before allocation: `q(sum of exact signed raw item values) == q(stored seed amount)`, and `q(sum of exact signed raw detail values) == q(stored credit-item amount)`. This is not raw rational equality: a legacy parent `0.30000000000000004` and children `0.1`, `0.2` agree at 30 cents and are valid. No epsilon is used and no raw value is normalized. Canonical-cent agreement is necessary but not sufficient: at every hierarchy level, check that the **inherited assigned target** satisfies the allocator feasibility bounds above. An infeasible target produces an integrity hold for the household, even when independently quantized raw parent and children agree. Do not decrement a floor, award above a ceiling, normalize source amounts, invent a balancing row, or substitute a different allocation algorithm to force equality. The live flip report counts such households as unassessable/integrity holds, never as unchanged. Legacy seeds with no items are valid opaque rows. Allocate `O` to seeds by seed id, each assigned seed target to its items by `sourceId`, then each credit-item target to its **non-additive** detail by provenance `sourceId`. Existing explanatory `unused`/`remainder`/`applied` detail without a record id uses a deterministic projection-only id from `[seed.id, creditItem.sourceId, detail.kind]`; each kind occurs at most once under that parent. A grouped detail uses the sorted constituent-id identity above. No new stored identifiers are required. Detail never contributes a second time to `O`. A provenance failure or parent/child mismatch that changes canonical cents produces an integrity hold with source ids for correction; no epsilon repair or fabricated rounding item is permitted. New carry records satisfy canonical cent conservation and exact projected equality.

Recompute allocations for the current eligible snapshot. A projected sub-cent row can move by a cent as eligible records enter/leave, while the stored amount remains unchanged. Largest remainder applies separately to additive bill, Service Credit, payment, disposition, billed/deferred charge and carry rows at their declared parent; it never mixes components or offsets Service Credits against money. Existing independently rounded **per-member bill displays** from #620 remain: two $33.33 member lines may sit under a $66.67 household subtotal; those lines do not promise additive equality. No separate rounding row is introduced.

## Public compatibility and release boundary

The owner's [Decision A](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/636#issuecomment-6755855758), [Decision B](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/636#issuecomment-6755898275) and [Decision C](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/636#issuecomment-6756101119) amend the earlier M4 scope record without erasing it. They approve the public contract, not implementation, a code head, merge or production operations. [The sharing spec](../../specs/sharing.md) owns detailed presentation and workflow acceptance; [M4](../../plans/629-cent-rounding-rollout.md#m4-both-public-writers-and-money-version-consumer-after-m3-and-638) owns the expanded source/split checkpoint.

Both public writers (`src/lib/share.js` through all creation/rebuild callers and `functions/billing.js` / `resolveShareToken`) derive I, B, P, R, N, T and balance from **one `projectHouseholdMoney` result for one household/source snapshot**. The adjusted annual-bills alias `combinedAnnualTotal` remains I; its monthly presentation never adds B/12. Show household **Usage charges billed** B and **Total owed** T; deferred pending charges remain token-member-grain and outside T. **Paid to date** / `totalPaid` becomes N, explained by **Payments received** P and one signed **Refunds / credit carried forward** R deduction. Omit B/R rows only at zero; never subtract R on the bill side or split/re-round it. Payment history allocates live rows to P, not N. Progress/callouts use T and N; T=0 has no percentage/division. Preserve signed balance/credit semantics without a new refund entitlement; U stays carry-only and #617 stays open/non-blocking.

For open/settling years, both writers derive `reopenedAdjustmentIds` with the board's existing `reopenedCreditAdjustmentIds` helper over complete household Refund Notice context. An unresolved `not_received` excludes its linked disposition from R; confirmed, unreported or resolved notices use normal eligibility, and cancelled dispositions remain excluded. Closed/archived years use null context, matching ADR 0007; late reports never reopen their money. Read year status with the money snapshot. Missing/loading/failed notice context is unavailable, never an empty or null fallback, including link generation and contact/name rebuilds. The Function adds `creditAdjustments` and an internal household notice read even without `refunds:read`; it discloses no notice records/ids/fields without that existing scope. Detailed notices stay member-scoped and live-only.

Decision B requires the `not_received` report, its resolution, disposition cancellation and year-close transitions to invalidate or rewrite the affected household's `publicShares` entries so **the next resolve goes live**, including reports while held. Preserve token expiry/revocation and access counting, sibling/year isolation and no year-document Admin writes. This is the sole targeted exception to existing cache-age timing; the stale-marker/deletion and concurrent-writer design remains a preimplementation gate in M4, not an approved algorithm or a new cancellation feature.

Both cache and Function responses carry `moneyProjectionVersion: 1` and `financialState: 'available' | 'unavailable'`. Available payloads carry the complete scoped money shape with checked numeric aliases from the authority. A valid-token unavailable result carries identity/year/scope context and independently authorized historical sections, **no projected money**: omit summary/bill money, paymentSummary, Service Credits, carried items, pending charges entirely and payment-history money. Render **Financial data unavailable**, **We can’t show current billing amounts. Please contact the administrator.** and **Try again**. Withhold pay links/QR, percentage/progress/status claims and bill-dependent Question This creation. Existing scoped Refund Notices retain their frozen stored amounts and receipt/report actions; Review Requests retain status/reply. Render these independently, with no whole-page early return; an absent historical amount stays absent. Add no private holds, causes, source record ids/raw values, provenance or tie keys; existing public ids are not expanded. Allocate before stripping private keys.

Validate version, state and required shape from **either** source before rendering money. Missing/old/unknown/malformed caches resolve live first with no legacy flash. Incompatible/malformed Function results or failed lookup produce recoverable load failure and no cached amount, distinct from valid-token unavailable and invalid/expired/revoked links. Reject late old-tab cache replacement again after self-healing. Remove stale optional monetary keys on merge-based self-healing and scope removal; re-labelling old money is insufficient. Carried-item presence, cache age or expiry fields do not establish compatibility; the money version is separate from billing-year rev/draft versions.

Try again re-reads the source without year repair, normalization or retrying a money write; existing cache self-healing and metric writes remain. Once the view observes a hold, only a compatible authoritative available response restores money, with no stale fallback after an unavailable/incompatible authoritative response. Apply a background unavailable response to the current view. Preserve other cache-age/background timing and claim parity only for the same snapshot. The Functions-first/old-client interval remains separately verified; the version marker cannot retrofit old clients.

#462 remains open until its own acceptance is assessed at a real head, with no new completion dependency. #564's future voided-refund record is outside this amendment; retain #462's whichever-lands-second public parity obligation. #614/#615/#616 remain unchanged.

Every financial PR in the series needs explicit human financial review of its exact head as well as the repository review gates. Merge of unused primitives may precede callers; **no partial financial dependency set is released**. Complete and review the set, deploy and verify Functions before the consuming Hosting release, and record the old/new-client interval and reload behavior. #603 remains blocked until its prerequisites and deployment gates pass; #614/#616 owner acknowledgments and staged rules gates remain separate.

Before Hosting, the owner must run or separately authorize a **read-only report over live billing years**, counting households whose settlement state **or displayed owed** differs between the current and proposed projections. Record the union count and each category (with overlap), years/households inspected, integrity holds/unassessable households (including a count of households held for non-conforming money fields, by component and with record ids), source snapshot time and compared revisions; the final release review considers this evidence. This approval does not authorize that production read, a migration, a financial-data write, a deploy or a future head. No live report has been authorized or run by adoption of this ADR.
