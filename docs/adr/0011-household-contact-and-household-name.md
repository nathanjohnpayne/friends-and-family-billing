# Household contact and household name

Accepted 2026-11-06 for [#528](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/528), the data-model half of the redesign's RD-01 and RD-06 ([#524](https://github.com/nathanjohnpayne/friends-and-family-billing/issues/524)).

## Context

A household has always been computed, not stored: one member's record carries `linkedMembers`, and the household is that member plus everyone listed (CONTEXT.md, ADR 0001). The UI called that member the "primary" and the others "linked members", and drew them as a parent with children. The redesign draws households as groups of peers. It also needs two things the model could not do: change which member holds the list without rebuilding the household by hand, and give a household a name.

## Decision

**The stored primary stays; the UI calls it the Household contact.** The household contact is a label, not a rank. It is where the household's shared settings live (its optional name) and who receives household-level actions (a Refund is issued to the contact, as before). No UI copy ranks one member over another.

**Changing the contact rewrites only the link list.** `BillingYearService.saveHousehold({ contactId, memberIds, householdName })` makes `contactId` the stored primary, sets its `linkedMembers` to the other ids, and clears `linkedMembers` on everyone else in the household. `changeHouseholdContact(memberId)` is the same edit with membership and name unchanged. Forming a household from individuals makes the chosen contact the primary. Members left out of `memberIds` become individuals. A member cannot be taken from another household. A household of only the contact is dissolved.

**No ledger row moves.** Payments and reversals, Credits and Refunds, carried opening balances, Service Credits and Usage Charges all stay on the member they were recorded for. This is safe because every household figure already sums over the whole member set (`[primary, ...linkedMembers]`, e.g. `getHouseholdFinancials`, `getHouseholdRecordedRefund`, `getHouseholdOpeningBalance`, `getHouseholdDeferredCharges`, `buildCarryForward`). The contact therefore changes which id keys a household, not what it owes, paid or holds in credit. The cent projection (ADR 0010) is defined over the same member set, so its integer-cent components are unchanged too.

**The contact cannot be removed without a replacement.** `removeMember` refuses a member who is a household contact; choose a new contact first. Household members who are not the contact can still be removed.

**The household name is optional and lives on the contact's record** as `familyMembers[].householdName`: a trimmed string of at most 60 characters. It moves to the new contact on a contact change, and it is dropped when the household is dissolved or a member stops being the contact. Without a stored name, a household is called by its members' shared surname, or the contact's surname when they do not share one; the UI appends "household". Member records are saved whole, so `buildSavePayload` (ADR 0008) carries the field without a new allowlist entry. `normalizeYearData` drops a blank or non-string value, and a name stored on any member who is not a household contact. `buildNewYearData` clones it onto contacts at Start New Year.

**Share links stay with their member.** Share tokens and cached `publicShares` documents are keyed by member id. After a contact change, the old contact's link becomes that member's own link (their own bills and payments) and the new contact's link shows the household. After its billing-year write succeeds, `saveHousehold` rebuilds the cached documents of both contacts' active links, exactly as `createAndPruneShareLink` builds them for each token's scopes. `rebuildMemberShareDocs` writes them in a transaction that rereads each token and skips one that was revoked meanwhile, so a revoked link's cache is never recreated, and it keeps each document's `accessCount` and `lastAccessedAt`. If the billing-year write fails, no share document is rebuilt. Other members' links do not change shape and are not rewritten. If the rebuild fails, the household change stays saved and the cache self-heals through `resolveShareToken` within the hour. Refund Notices keep the `memberId` they were issued to (recipient-bound, #542).

**A contact change is audited** with a `HOUSEHOLD_CONTACT_CHANGED` billing event.

**Household operations are confirmed, serialized and year-bound.** `saveHousehold` waits for its own billing-year write to succeed before rebuilding share documents. On failure it reverts only the household fields it changed, removes only its own event, enqueues a compensating save so a write queued meanwhile cannot persist the rejected change, waits for it, and reports the error (saying so, and asking for a reload, if the compensating save fails too). When the local state no longer holds the edit (the year was reloaded while the write was pending) or the year is now read-only, it waits for the write queued behind it and then, in a transaction against the stored year document, reverts only this edit's household fields and event, leaving every other stored field (status, lifecycle events, ledger rows, changes from another client) as it is. If the year was switched while the write was pending, it leaves the current (other) year alone and compensates the edited year's stored document the same way. Household operations run one at a time, each bound to the user and year it was requested in (rejected if either changed before its turn), with share-rebuild inputs snapshotted before the write. While one is pending, other link-graph edits (`updateMember` with `linkedMembers`, `removeMember`) are refused, so a rollback never has to merge a competing graph change, and so are mutations that read the household to decide who a ledger row belongs to (`issueRefund`, a distributed `recordPayment`, `billDeferredCharges`), so no row is built from a household that is then rolled back. Start New Year (`createYear`) and year status changes (`setYearStatus`) are refused too: the rollover snapshots the household into the new year and the prior year's carry marking, and a status write carries the in-memory events (and closing the year would stop the compensating save). The lock is symmetric: while Start New Year (including its switch into the new year) or a status change is running, household operations and link-graph edits are refused, so they never snapshot a year whose status, events or carry markings are about to change.

## Consequences

- Promotion is a pure link-graph edit with a provable invariant (household totals equal before and after), covered by `tests/react/lib/household.test.js`.
- The carry seed for a new year is keyed by whoever is the contact at Start New Year (`coadj_<year>_<primaryMemberId>`). After a mid-year contact change it is keyed by the new contact. The amount is the household's, so nothing is lost or doubled.
- The UI screens that edit households in full (the Edit household dialog with a contact choice and name field, #531) and draw households as peers (#529) build on `saveHousehold` and `getHouseholdName`. This change adds only the label, neutral copy, and a "Make household contact" action.
